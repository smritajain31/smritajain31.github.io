Hi, I'm Smrita.

Product Design | AI & Conversational UX | Design Systems | Enterprise Digital Platforms

Award-winning Creative and Experience Design professional with directorial and senior-level experience at firms including The Aquario Group and MRM (now Omnicom). I bring 20+ years of design experience, including 7+ years in product design, where I was the founding product designer for Four Seasons Yacht and created a booking platform that generated over $66 million in revenue. That product, built over nearly 4 years, is now running successfully and expanding into several verticals, growing with immense speed and clarity on a strong foundation. I also established 100+ design components for Coca-Cola and created a system that brought 500+ brands together into one single design system. I now create agentic AI workflows, design rules, and governance around AI agents most recent case study is for HarborGate, in which 7 agents work autonomously (with defined limits), saving 30 days of onboarding and verification time and reducing it to 2 to 4 days.

No AI model was used to write this. 100% human.

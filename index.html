Hi, I'm Smrita.

Senior Product Designer · Artist · Educator · Founder

I’m a Brooklyn-based designer, artist, photographer, author and educator. Born in New Delhi, India 🇮🇳, I’ve always been curious about the stories people carry—and how design and art can make those stories visible.

As the founder of The Aquario Group, I work across product design, strategy and visual storytelling. My creative practice also reaches into cultural documentary photography 📷, painting 🎨 and writing ✍️. I authored and designed Creating Durga, a photography book exploring the making of the Durga festival.

I love helping people find clarity in complicated things, and sharing what I’ve learned with the next generation of designers. ✨ Thank you for being here!

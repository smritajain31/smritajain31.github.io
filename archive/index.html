About this archive

SJ, Smrita's research agent, searches the web twice a month for anything published by or about her and The Aquario Group, confirms it is really about her, and adds a short factual entry that links to the original. Every link is re-checked weekly; when a page disappears, SJ points to an archived copy.

  • Verified SJ opened the original source and confirmed it.
  • Own site Stated on Smrita's own websites.
  • Cited Listed on her CV or Wikipedia; the original has not been re-checked yet.

Entry descriptions are short summaries written by SJ. Smrita's own words live on the home page. Spotted something missing or wrong? Email Smrita.
